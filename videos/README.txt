HOW TO MAKE YOUR CAPCUT VIDEO PERMANENT:
1. Export your video from CapCut:
   - Format: MP4
   - Resolution: 1080p (or 720p)
   - Tip: Both widescreen (16:9) and smartphone vertical (9:16) 
     are automatically supported with smart framing!
2. Rename your exported file to:
   our-story.mp4

3. Place it right in this folder:
   e:\Projects\Birthday\videos\our-story.mp4

✨ LIVE IN-BROWSER PICKER & AUTO-SAVE:
You can also simply click the "📹 Load CapCut Video" button directly 
on the cinema player in your browser. The website will automatically 
store your video inside your browser's persistent IndexedDB storage, 
so it stays loaded and ready across page reloads!

🎬 AUTO-PLAY EXPERIENCE:
As soon as you scroll down to the "Moments in Motion" section, 
the video automatically begins playing with cinematic ambient glow! 
Tap the floating "Tap for Sound ♫" pill anytime for full romantic sound.
